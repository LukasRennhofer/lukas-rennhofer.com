I'm a curious and driven software developer from Austria, currently studying computer science at HTL Wiener Neustadt, where I focus on building a strong foundation in both theoretical and practical aspects of programming. Alongside my studies, I'm an active member of the robo4you robotics club, where I enjoy working on a realtime-based ecosystem for robotics competitions.

My main interests lie in writing efficient, high-performance code and understanding how things work at a deeper level. I'm particularly drawn to areas like computer graphics and parallel systems programming, including writing shaders and working with GPUs, where performance, memory management, and hardware interaction play a critical role.

I've also contributed to the Blender project, where I worked on bug fixes and improvements for the EEVEE rendering engine. This experience gave me valuable insight into large-scale, real-world codebases and collaborative development. Looking ahead, I'll be joining NOVOMATIC AG as an intern in the summer of 2026, where I'll be working on biometric systems.
